Gear Puzzle Sorter

Photo or camera → any-color, any-grid board · fail-closed if unreadable

1. Scan the board

Use a clear, top-down photo of the full board — one row of pegs or two Labor Day / Gear Sort shelves. If the read is weak, you get an error — the app will not invent a board.

2. Chip / peg editor

No board yet

Upload a photo or use the camera.

3. Play the sort

Each step pours the top run of matching chips onto another peg. Reset returns to the scanned or edited start.

Runs entirely on your device. Photos are never uploaded.
